Overview · Privacy · Support · 简体中文

Navigate long conversations

Find the messages you need in long ChatGPT and Gemini conversations.

Timeline and search

Navigate messages in order and search loaded content with matching text shown in context.

Bookmarks and sections

Mark important messages, expand real reply headings, and return to your previous reading position.

Local and multilingual

Choose from eight interface languages. Chat text stays in the current tab's memory; settings and bookmark markers are saved locally.

Works on chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, and gemini.google.com. The extension does not automatically load all history or scan your account's other conversations.

Use and supportPrivacy policy

Position fix in v1.8.1

The outline launcher and timeline rail move down by 64 pixels to leave room for the website's download, fullscreen, and other top controls. Reload the extension and refresh conversation pages to apply the update.

Browser installation

The repository contains a Manifest V3 source package for Microsoft Edge and Chrome. Load the extension folder through edge://extensions/ or chrome://extensions/ in developer mode, then refresh ChatGPT or Gemini. Website publication does not mean a store submission has been certified.
